Create School Form 4

Monthly learner movement and attendance

Review the figures before downloading. Enrollment uses the current active roster for this school year. Attendance counts one present/late mark per learner per Manila calendar day from closed, synchronized sessions; excused marks are excluded. For past months, changing enrollment, incomplete sessions, or subject-only records, enter verified SF2 figures. Confirm the advisory teacher for each section.

Enter male and female learner-days (the sum of daily attendance), plus verified dropout and transfer counts. Where dated movement records exist, their counts are suggested; review earlier history before accepting them. Enter 0 when none. Daily averages, percentages, M/F totals, cumulative movement, and grade summaries are calculated automatically.
