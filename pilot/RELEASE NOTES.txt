EduScan Teacher Pilot 0.2.0
===========================

Purpose: supervised single-machine teacher pilot on Windows.

Included:
- Offline-capable PWA and QR camera/image scanner
- Local FastAPI service and SQLite database
- Teacher, IT administrator, and principal roles
- Class distribution and automatic roster updates
- Optional student photographs
- QR issue, revoke, replace, and eight-card A4 printing
- Attendance schedules, synchronization queue, reports, audit history
- School-year closing and controlled student promotion
- Guided setup, start, stop, and backup commands

This is a pilot release, not the final multi-machine school deployment.
Version: 0.2.0
